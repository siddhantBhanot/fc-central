# Retail Credit Underwriting & Risk Appraisal

## 01. 5 Cs of Credit & Retail Risk Governance
- **Summary**: Foundational appraisal using Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, and Conditions to build defensible credit sanction memorandums.
- **Context**:
  - lessons/01-credit-principles/01-five-cs-framework.md
  - lessons/01-credit-principles/02-regulatory-risk-caps.md
- **Knowledge Check**:
  - **Question**: When evaluating an unsecured personal loan applicant, which of the 5 Cs of credit carries the single highest statistical correlation with repayment default?
  - **Type**: multiple_choice
  - **Options**:
    - Collateral adequacy
    - [x] Character (repayment track record and credit bureau history)
    - Macroeconomic Conditions in applicant's industry
    - Paid-up Equity Capital
  - **Explanation**: In unsecured retail credit where no collateral exists, 'Character'—evidenced by historical bureau performance, absence of write-offs, and disciplined past obligations—is the foremost predictor of default.

## 02. Income Assessment: Salaried vs Self-Employed Profiles
- **Summary**: Techniques for evaluating Form 16, net take-home salary, cyclic business turnover, profit and loss adjustments, and GST-to-banking reconciliations.
- **Context**:
  - lessons/02-income-appraisal/01-salaried-underwriting.md
  - lessons/02-income-appraisal/02-self-employed-business-cashflows.md
- **Knowledge Check**:
  - **Question**: Why do credit underwriters add back depreciation and non-cash expenses when calculating the cash accruals of a self-employed borrower?
  - **Type**: multiple_choice
  - **Options**:
    - To artificially boost the borrower's taxable income for tax filing
    - [x] Because depreciation is an accounting book entry that does not represent an actual physical outflow of liquid cash
    - Because RBI guidelines mandate counting balance sheet machinery as cash
    - To offset statutory interest dues to commercial suppliers
  - **Explanation**: Cash profit is computed as Net Profit + Non-cash expenses (such as depreciation and amortisation). Since depreciation does not drain actual cash reserves, it is available for debt servicing.

## 03. Debt Burden Ratios: FOIR, LTV & Leverage Thresholds
- **Summary**: Calculating Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR), Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits under RBI home loan regulations, and interest rate sensitivity stress testing.
- **Context**:
  - lessons/03-risk-metrics/01-foir-ltv-computations.md
  - lessons/03-risk-metrics/02-stress-testing-repayment.md
- **Knowledge Check**:
  - **Question**: According to RBI prudential regulations for individual housing loans, what is the maximum permissible Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio for a loan ticket size exceeding INR 75 Lakhs?
  - **Type**: multiple_choice
  - **Options**:
    - 90%
    - 85%
    - [x] 75%
    - 60%
  - **Explanation**: Per RBI regulatory ceilings, loans up to ₹30 Lakh can have up to 90% LTV; ₹30 Lakh to ₹75 Lakh have up to 80% LTV; and loans exceeding ₹75 Lakh are capped at a maximum of 75% LTV.

## 04. Forensic Statement Analysis & Red Flags
- **Summary**: Identifying red flags in applicant banking: round-tripping, non-genuine salary narrations, frequent outward cheque returns, and sudden unexplained credits before application.
- **Context**:
  - lessons/04-forensic-analysis/01-bank-statement-red-flags.md
  - lessons/04-forensic-analysis/02-mitigation-and-sanction-conditions.md
- **Knowledge Check**:
  - **Question**: Which of the following banking transaction patterns in an applicant's primary operating account represents the most severe credit fraud red flag?
  - **Type**: multiple_choice
  - **Options**:
    - Occasional ATM cash withdrawals over weekends
    - Utility bill payments made through third-party payment gateways
    - [x] Circular transactions where identical round-sum amounts are transferred between sister entities and reversed within 24 hours
    - Salary credited via NEFT on the 1st day of the month
  - **Explanation**: Circular transfers of round amounts among related entities within hours indicate synthetic turnover inflation or artificial balance fabrication to deceive lending algorithms.
